personal-assistant

A private assistant agent built and operated by Alexander Ledovsky for his own use. It reads and manages his own Google Calendar, Gmail, Contacts and Google Drive through the Google Workspace APIs. The app is not distributed and has no other users.

Contact: alexander.ledovsky@gmail.com

Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-09-05

Data accessed. With the account owner's explicit OAuth consent, the app accesses Google Calendar events, Gmail messages, Google Contacts and directory data, and Google Drive files of that account.

Use. Data is used only to answer the account owner's own requests, such as listing upcoming meetings or summarizing recent mail. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage. OAuth tokens are stored on machines controlled by the operator. Retrieved data is processed to answer a request and is not retained beyond that.

Google API Services User Data Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
